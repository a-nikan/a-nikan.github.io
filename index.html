Task Reminder

Task Reminder is a task management and reminder application for Windows and Android. Its purpose is to help you organize your daily tasks, set due dates and times, and receive reliable notifications so nothing is forgotten.

What the app does

  • Create, edit, complete, and delete tasks with categories
  • Set due dates and reminder times with local notifications
  • Works fully offline — your data is stored on your own device
  • Optional, user-initiated sync of your data across your own devices through your personal Google Drive
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking

Platforms

Available as a Windows desktop installer and an Android APK.
