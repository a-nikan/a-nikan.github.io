Privacy Policy for Task Reminder

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Task Reminder is a task and reminder application for Windows and Android. This policy explains what information the app stores, how it is used, and what it never does. The app operates entirely on your devices; there are no developer-operated servers that receive your data.

2. Information the app stores

The app stores the tasks and reminders you create (titles, notes, categories, dates, times, completion status), your app settings, and your notification preferences. This data is stored in a local database file on your device.

3. Optional cloud sync

If you choose to enable sync, the app uses Google Sign-In with the drive.appdata Google API scope to read and write a single sync file inside the appdata folder of your own Google Drive. The synced copy of your tasks exists only in your personal Drive account. You can disconnect sync at any time from the app settings, which stops all cloud access immediately.

4. What the app never does

  • Does not sell, share, or transmit your personal data to third parties
  • Contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs
  • Does not read any files in your Google Drive other than its own sync file
  • Does not require any account registration with the developer

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete it inside the app or uninstall the app. To delete the cloud copy, disconnect sync and delete the file named taskreminder-db.json from your Google Drive app data (or clear the app's access from your Google Account settings at myaccount.google.com).

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
